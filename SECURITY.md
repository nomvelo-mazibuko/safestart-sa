# Security policy

SafeStart SA collects no personal data and has no user accounts.

## Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem or a mistake in the legal information, please tell us privately first:

- Email: **https://safestart-sa.onrender.com/**

Please include what you found and how to reproduce it. Do not post it publicly until it has been fixed.
We will aim to reply within 7 days.

## What is in scope

- The link checker (`verifier.py`) and its safety limits
- Wrong or outdated labour-law information
- Anything that could expose a user's information

The app gives general information, not legal advice. Results are warning signs, not proof.
